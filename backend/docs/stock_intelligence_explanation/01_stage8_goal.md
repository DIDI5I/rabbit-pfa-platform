# Stage 8 Goal

The goal of Stage 8 is to let the chatbot explain the backend numerical Stock Intelligence Engine.

The chatbot can now answer questions like:

```text
why should I reorder ASM-PMP-XR200?
explain stock intelligence for ASM-PMP-XR200
what model was used for CMP-BRG-6205?
why is confidence LOW?
why is this HIGH priority?
```

This is not free-form AI reasoning.

The explanation is grounded in the existing endpoint:

```text
GET /stock/intelligence/reorder-recommendations
```
