# Stage 7 Goal

The goal is to prevent future AI/API summarization from inventing joins between product modules.

Instead, Rabbit builds a backend-controlled product snapshot first, then the chatbot presents it.

Tool:

```text
product_intelligence_snapshot
```

V1 sections:

```text
product
product_relationships
reorder owner-only
data_quality
```

Future sections:

```text
commercial ratings/promotions
stock detail
procurement
cost
```
