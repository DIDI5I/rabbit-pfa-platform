# Stage 9B Goal

The goal of Stage 9B was to make the Product Intelligence Snapshot more complete by adding commercial catalogue signals.

Before Stage 9B, the snapshot included:

```text
product
product_relationships
reorder owner-only
data_quality
```

After Stage 9B, it also includes:

```text
commercial
```

The commercial section helps answer:

```text
Is this product promoted?
Does it have reviews?
What is its average rating?
Is it commercially attractive?
```
