# Stage 9C Goal

Before Stage 9C, the chatbot could explain stock intelligence for one product using:

```text
stock_intelligence_explanation
```

Stage 9C adds dashboard-level visibility and explanation.

The chatbot can now answer:

```text
show stock intelligence summary
show reorder dashboard
explain stock intelligence dashboard
what models are being used in stock intelligence?
why is most confidence ESTIMATE_ONLY?
why are there 3 reorder recommendations?
```

This completes a stronger owner-facing stock intelligence workflow.
